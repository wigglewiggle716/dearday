Who we are

More than a place to buy a gift.

Dear Day is a platform for planning meaningful occasions from start to finish. We bring together the details that usually require separate searches and bookings — the venue or experience, gift, cake and sweets, and flowers — in one experience you can shape around your style and budget.

Our goal is not to overwhelm you with more options. It is to make the right options easier to find. We curate carefully, make the details clear, and help every part of the day feel connected.

We started in Cairo and Giza, but Dear Day was built to grow with every kind of day worth celebrating — a birthday, anniversary, engagement, proposal, or simply a day you want to make feel more special.

From separate details to one beautifully connected day.
