Privacy at Dear Day

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Dear Day may collect, why we use it, how it may be shared when necessary to provide the service, and the choices available to you.

By using Dear Day, creating an account, or placing an order or booking, you acknowledge this policy. Some information is necessary in order to provide the service you request.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Dear Day website and digital services, including account creation, occasion planning, product or service selection, orders, bookings and customer support. Some products and services are fulfilled by independent partners, and only information reasonably required to fulfil the order or booking may be shared with them.

2. Data We May Collect

Depending on how you use Dear Day, we may collect account and contact details, delivery or occasion location, occasion preferences, order and booking history, customer-support communications, and technical information such as device, browser, IP address and cookies where used.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use information to create and manage accounts, save selections, fulfil orders and bookings, coordinate with partners, communicate order status, process payments and refunds, provide support, prevent fraud and misuse, improve the service, and send marketing only where permitted.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share only the information required with the relevant partner, delivery provider, payment provider, technical service provider or professional adviser. Dear Day does not sell personal data to advertisers.

5. Payment Information

Payment transactions may be processed by an approved payment provider. Dear Day should not store complete card credentials where the payment provider processes them directly.

6. Cookies & Technical Data

Cookies or similar technologies may be used for essential site functions, session continuity, security, preferences, analytics or other uses disclosed through the site where applicable.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the service, legal, accounting, security, refund or dispute purposes. Data that no longer needs to identify you may be deleted or de-identified.

8. Data Security

Dear Day applies reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their credentials and report suspected unauthorised access.

9. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You may ask to correct account information or request permanent account and personal-data deletion. Signed-in customers can submit a deletion request from My Account. The request is reviewed before deletion is completed, and historical records that must be retained are de-identified where appropriate.

10. Children

Dear Day is not intended to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. Where applicable, an adult should place and manage the order.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the service, legal requirements or operational practices change. The current version and update date will be published on this page.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions, corrections or support, use the Contact Us page. For account deletion, see Account & Data Deletion.
