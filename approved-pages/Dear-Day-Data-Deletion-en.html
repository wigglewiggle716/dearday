Account & Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Dear Day account and personal data associated with it. This page explains how to submit a request and what happens after your identity is verified.

These instructions also apply if you created or accessed your Dear Day account using Facebook or another social sign-in provider.

1. How to request deletion

  1. Sign in to your Dear Day account.
  2. Open My Account.
  3. Go to Account & data deletion and choose Request account deletion.
  4. Your request is recorded and a Super Admin is notified for review.
  5. If approved, Dear Day permanently removes the sign-in account and personal profile data and de-identifies historical records that must be retained for operational, accounting, legal, refund, or dispute purposes.
Open My Account

2. What data is deleted?

After verification, we delete or de-identify personal data that is no longer needed to provide the service. This may include account details, contact information, preferences, and data associated with your use of Dear Day.

3. Data we may need to retain

We may retain limited records where required to comply with legal or accounting obligations, protect legal rights, or resolve an active order, refund, charge, or dispute. Data retained for these purposes is not used for marketing.

4. Facebook Login

If you used Facebook to create or access your Dear Day account, follow the same steps above to request deletion of data held by Dear Day. Removing Dear Day from your Facebook settings by itself does not automatically submit a deletion request for data stored by Dear Day.

5. Privacy questions

For more information about how Dear Day handles personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
